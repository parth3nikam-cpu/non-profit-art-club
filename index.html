Why we’re here

Art belongs to all of us.

For some people art is just something that exists in their day to day life, but for others, it is how they process emotions and express their feelings. Although not everyone has this opportunity. They may have grown up in an environment where they didn’t have the supplies or the money to create. We believe that everyone deserves a chance to freely express creativity.

The power of making

Art changes things

Small creative moments can make a meaningful difference.

01 / Stress relief

45 minutes of art can lower stress.

In a study of 39 adults, one art-making session significantly lowered participants’ cortisol levels—regardless of their previous art experience.

Drexel University research ↗
02 / Mental well-being

76% of Americans say the arts are crucial to their well-being.

The same national survey found that three in four Americans would follow a doctor’s recommendation to participate in the arts to improve physical or mental health.

Americans for the Arts survey ↗
03 / Human history

Art has been part of us for at least 67,800 years.

An ancient hand stencil found in Sulawesi, Indonesia, is the oldest known cave art—proof that people have been leaving creative marks for tens of thousands of years.

Archaeological discovery ↗
